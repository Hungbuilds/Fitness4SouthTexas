Locally Owned & Operated

A Cotulla Gym Built Around Real Coaching

Fitness4 has been locally owned and operated in Cotulla since 2018, with a focus that's a little different from a typical gym: one-on-one personal training, including programs built specifically for older adults and people who are new to strength training.

Members get unrestricted 24/7 access on top of that — so the gym fits the schedule, not the other way around. Fitness4 also supports the Cotulla Cowboys Powerlifting Team, sponsoring local athletes on their way to state competition.

"We're not just a gym — we're a community dedicated to your wellness journey."
